Climate Hackers

A monthly meetup for anyone wanting to build towards a greener future.

Overview

Climate Hackers is a Boston-based event series for anyone who is climate-curious, wants to build practical solutions for a greener future, or is looking for community and collaboration in the city.

Quick Facts

  • When: First week of December 2026 (TBD)
  • Where: Boston/Cambridge/Somerville, MA (venue TBD)
  • Cost: Free. Donations accepted
  • Who: Anyone in the Boston area who wants to work with like-minded folks to build solutions for a greener future

Who is this for?

The event is open for anyone with an interest and motivation to collaborate to build climate solutions.

Whether you are a professional working in the climate space, a student, a tech worker, or someone who simply cares about the environment and has skills and ideas to offer, you are welcome.

  • You do not have to know how to code or work in tech to attend
  • No prior experience necessary
  • All ages, genders, ethnicities, religions, beliefs, and backgrounds are invited to join

Guiding Principles

  • Focus on building long-term, cross-disciplinary projects and collaborations
  • Focus on local, grassroots communities and solutions
  • Create space for climate-conscious and climate-curious individuals in the Boston-area to come together in collaboration

How to Prepare?

No prior experience in hackathons, tech, or climate work is required. Participants should plan to bring a device (e.g. laptop, tablet) to work on and any other additional tools needed to do the work.

Topics & Tracks

Any topic related to climate-adjacent problems is welcome, though here are some potential starting points to explore. This list is subject to change.

  • Clean energy & energy data
  • Supporting existing grassroots non-profit climate orgs in the region
  • Environmental justice
  • Municipal open data (e.g. City of Boston, Mass DOT)
  • Permacomputing (hardware)
  • Solar + solar-powered IoT & devices (e.g. balcony solar, solar powerbanks, etc.)
  • Zero-waste
  • Ocean tech
  • Local gov't initiative exploration (e.g. BERDO, MassSave)
  • Urban gardening, food justice
  • Public transit/transportation
  • Urban flora & fauna
  • Climate education
  • Solarpunk (art, literature, aesthetics)
  • More to come...

FAQs

  • Are there judges & winners? For this hackathon, there are no judges or winners. The goal is to collectively build a set of tools to support local climate initiatives.
  • How long will it be? Roughly 4hrs, though details are TBD
  • Will there be a prize? The prize is the friendship made along the way :)

AI Policy

We can all agree that the rapid deployment of artificial intelligence and the resources needed to power this infrastrucutre stands to have potential negative effects on our environment.

Despite this, Climate Hackers policy is that of technical pragmatism. AI, like many revolutionary technologies before it, is here to stay. What we can do, however, is use it for good to support projects, organizations & fields that may otherwise may blocked by lack of resources.

That said, "AI slop" is not allowed. Our definition of this is something like a haphazardly vibecoded web app for an issue without much research behind it. (More to come)...
