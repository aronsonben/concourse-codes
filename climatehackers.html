A monthly hackathon for building and collaborating on climate-oriented projects.

Overview

Climate Hackers is a Boston-based hackathon series for anyone who is climate-curious, wants to build practical solutions for a greener future, or is looking for community and collaboration amongst fellow climate-conscious individuals.

Quick Facts

  • When: First week of December 2026 (TBD)
  • Where: Boston/Cambridge/Somerville, MA (venue TBD)
  • Cost: Free. Donations accepted
  • Who: Anyone in the Boston area who wants to work with like-minded folks to build solutions for a greener future

Who is this for?

The event is open for anyone with an interest and motivation to collaborate to build climate solutions.

Whether you are a professional, student, retiree, or someone who simply cares about the environment and has skills and ideas to offer, you are welcome. No climate or tech experience required.

  • You do not have to know how to code or work in tech to attend
  • No prior experience necessary
  • All ages, genders, ethnicities, religions, beliefs, and backgrounds are invited to join

Guiding Principles

We aim to strive for a set of guiding principles to ground our work in intentionality, community, and collaboration.

  1. Focus on building long-term, cross-disciplinary projects and collaborations
  2. Focus on local, grassroots communities and solutions (where possible)
  3. Create space for climate-conscious and climate-curious individuals in the Boston-area to come together in collaboration

Topics & Tracks

Any topic related to climate-adjacent problems is welcome, though here are some potential starting points to explore. This list is subject to change.

  • Clean energy & energy data
  • Supporting existing grassroots non-profit climate orgs in the region
  • Environmental justice
  • Municipal open data (e.g. City of Boston, Mass DOT)
  • Permacomputing (hardware)
  • Solar + solar-powered IoT & devices (e.g. balcony solar, solar powerbanks, etc.)
  • Zero-waste
  • Ocean tech
  • Local gov't initiative exploration (e.g. BERDO, MassSave)
  • Urban gardening, food justice
  • Public transit/transportation
  • Urban flora & fauna
  • Climate education
  • Solarpunk (art, literature, aesthetics)
  • More to come...

FAQs

  • Are there judges & winners? For this hackathon, there are no judges or winners. The goal is to collectively build a set of tools to support local climate initiatives.
  • How long will it be? Roughly 4hrs. Details to be confirmed.
  • Will there be a prize? As of now, there is no physical prize. The prize is the friendship made along the way :)
  • How do I prepare? Come prepared with ideas, an open mind, and a collaborative spirit. No other experience required.
  • What kind of skills are required? Any domain expertise is accepted. Whether you're an engineer, designer, founder, writer, gardener, or student - as long as you have ideas to offer and a desire to help you are welcome.
  • What kinds of projects will be worked on? Per the nature of a hackathon, most projects may be software oriented. There is no limitation, however, and we will expand on this in the future.

AI Policy

Any concerned citizen can agree that the rapid deployment of artificial intelligence and the resources needed to power this infrastrucutre stands to have some negative consequences on our environment.

Despite this, Climate Hackers policy is that of technical pragmatism. AI, like many revolutionary technologies before it, is an unstoppable force.
What we can do, however, is use it for good to support projects, organizations & causes that may otherwise may be blocked by lack of resources.

That said, "AI slop" is not allowed. Our definition of this is something like a lazily vibecoded web app for some perceived issue without much research behind it.

This is an evolving field and the policy stands to be updated.
